What Zero Hype believes:

Most B2B marketing problems have a specific failure point. Not "your messaging is weak" — that's a symptom. The failure point is usually one of: wrong ICP, vague goal, mismatched channel, weak post-click, or a measurement system that tracks the wrong things.

AI will automate your mediocrity faster. Broken strategy at scale, with better formatting. The answer is not a better prompt. It's a clearer diagnosis.

The best outreach anyone has ever seen was two sentences. No one has ever said that about a 200-word email. That's not a philosophy. That's a pattern from 20+ years of data.
