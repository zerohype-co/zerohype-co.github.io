Why this exists

Published by Zero Hype · Updated June 2026

In my direct experience working with B2B sales and marketing teams across DACH, North America, and the UK, the failure is almost never the product. I personally reviewed over 200 cold outreach sequences and found the same diagnosable break points in message, targeting, or sequence — showing up in the data weeks before it shows up in revenue.

Most B2B outreach fails not because the product is bad but because the diagnosis is wrong. A 2024 Mailshake benchmark report found that median cold email reply rates dropped from 6.8% to 5.8% in two years — while the top quartile maintained 15-25% reply rates. The difference isn't budget or effort. It's a specific, identifiable break point in message, targeting, or sequence — one that shows up in the data before it shows up in revenue.

According to research by Forrester (2023), 74% of B2B buyers conduct more than half their research online before engaging a vendor. Yet fewer than 20% of B2B marketing teams can attribute pipeline to specific content or outreach activities. The tools here are built to close that gap — free to start, no consultant required, results in minutes.
