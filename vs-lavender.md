## The problem Lavender can't fix

Lavender is genuinely useful for one thing: writing cold emails that don't sound like cold emails. It catches passive voice, flags overly long sentences, suggests more natural personalization. If your email reads like a template, Lavender helps.

But here's what we see consistently: B2B teams invest in better email writing while the real problem is that they're emailing the wrong people about the wrong thing. A beautifully written email to someone who has zero reason to care still gets ignored. Lavender can't fix your ICP. It can't fix your positioning. It can't tell you that cold email might not even be the right channel for your deal size.

## Questions

### Should I use both Lavender and Zero Hype?

If your strategy is solid but your writing is weak, start with Lavender. If your strategy is broken, fix it first — otherwise you're optimizing something that's already pointing the wrong direction. Zero Hype first, then Lavender.

### Zero Hype doesn't integrate with my email tool. Is that a problem?

Intentionally not. Zero Hype is an async diagnostic, not an inline assistant. The audit happens once, delivers a framework you apply across all your emails. The value is in the diagnosis, not the real-time suggestion.

### My open rates are fine but reply rates are terrible. Which helps?

Reply rate is a strategy problem, not a writing problem. Good subject line, bad offer or wrong ICP. This is exactly what the Outreach Autopsy diagnoses: what's breaking between open and reply.
