## Why automation makes bad strategy worse

The most common pattern we see: a team invests in Reply.io or Lemlist, builds a 7-step sequence, and sends 500 emails. 2% reply. They A/B test subject lines for 3 weeks. Still 2%. They upgrade the plan. Still 2%.

The problem isn't the tool. It's that the ICP is too broad, the email talks about the product instead of the prospect's problem, and the offer is unclear. Reply.io can't fix any of that. It just runs the sequence you gave it.

## Questions

### Can I use Reply.io and Zero Hype together?

Yes, and that's the right order. Get the audit first, fix the strategy and copy, then automate with Reply.io. Running audited sequences through automation is a much better bet than automating something broken.

### My Reply.io sequences have good open rates but no replies. What's wrong?

Open rate is a subject line metric. Reply rate is a strategy metric. High opens, low replies means your targeting is decent but your email body, offer, or ICP is off. That's exactly what the Outreach Autopsy diagnoses.

### Zero Hype is one-time. Reply.io is recurring. Which is more valuable?

Depends where the problem is. A one-time audit that fixes your positioning pays for itself in 1 good deal. Paying \$200/month for automation on a broken strategy is a recurring loss. Start with diagnosis.
